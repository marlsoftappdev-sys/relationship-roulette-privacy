Privacy Policy — Relationship Roulette

App: Relationship Roulette (com.marlsoft.relationshiproulette)
Effective date: 5 November 2026
Developer: Marlsoft LTD

The short version: the app collects nothing, transmits nothing and shares nothing. It has no internet permission, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Everything you type stays in the app's private storage on your own phone.

What the app stores on your device

So that a game can survive closing the app, Relationship Roulette saves the following in its own private app storage, which only the app can read:

  • the player names you type in (nicknames of your choosing)
  • custom editions and tasks you create
  • the tasks you have selected for the current wheel
  • your chosen theme and whether sound is muted
  • recent spin history and the current round

None of this leaves your device. We do not operate servers, so there is nowhere for it to be uploaded.

What the app does not do

  • It does not hold the internet permission, so it is technically incapable of sending your data off the device.
  • It does not access location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera.
  • It does not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or attribution services.
  • It does not create accounts or ask for an email address.

Purchases

The app is sold as a paid download through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google. We never receive your card details, billing address or Google account information. Google's handling of that data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

All data lives only on your device. You can remove it whenever you like:

  • Start Over inside the app clears the current round.
  • Android Settings → Apps → Relationship Roulette → Storage → Clear storage removes everything saved.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of it permanently.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds a feature that changes any of the above — for example, optional online play — this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date will change.
